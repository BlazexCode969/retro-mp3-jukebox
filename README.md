## 🎵 How It Works: Continuous Party Playback

Built like an authentic commercial jukebox, the party never encounters an awkward silence:

*   **Vinyl Ambience Selection:** Upon launch, a retro vinyl-styled selector lets you choose your base playlist (e.g., *80s Classics, Rock Hits, Jazz Bar*).
*   **Auto-Pilot Background Shuffle:** Music starts instantly. The player shuffles your chosen playlist, subtly badging these tracks with a rotation symbol (`⟳`) in the queue to maintain a non-stop room ambience.
*   **Interactive Priority Queue ("Insert Coin" Feel):** When a guest browses the library and taps OK:
    *   💥 **Jackpot Neon Burst:** Perimeter neon tubes and speaker grilles erupt in an energetic double-flash, confirming the track selection.
    *   ⭐ **Golden Star Priority:** The chosen track receives a gold badge (`★`) and jumps to the front of the queue, taking immediate priority over background tracks.
*   **Seamless Fade & Transition:** Once all user-selected priority songs finish, Blazex Jukebox seamlessly falls back to background shuffle mode without missing a beat.

## 🌟 Key Features & Capabilities

### 1. "Golden Audio Pack" (Audiophile Sound Support)
Enjoy complete format freedom without compression compromises:
*   **Lossless Studio Audio:** Full native playback for FLAC and WAV (bit-perfect CD sound quality).
*   **Versatile Standard Formats:** Fast, lightweight playback for massive MP3 and WMA collections.
*   **Automated Sorting:** Folders are instantly indexed in clean alphabetical order regardless of extension.

### 2. Dynamic Neon Light Choreography (4 Animated Modes)
Unlike flat media players, Blazex features an integrated light sequencer with 4 distinct real-time lighting programs:
*   **Volcano:** Lights climb symmetrically from the base, meet at the arch apex, and wash back down.
*   **Rock 'n' Roll Swing:** High-energy alternating flash between side pillars and top corners.
*   **Accumulation:** Sequentially charges the entire cabinet arch until it glows at peak power.
*   **Classic Ping-Pong:** Smooth, hypnotic neon chase gliding endlessly across the frame.
